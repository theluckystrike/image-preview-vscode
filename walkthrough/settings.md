## Settings

- `imgPreview.aliases`: your own prefixes, for example `{ "@images": "src/assets/images" }`.
- `imgPreview.publicFolders`: folders searched for paths that start with `/`.
- `imgPreview.hoverMaxSize`: the longest side of the hover image, in pixels.
- `imgPreview.gutter` and `imgPreview.hover`: turn either part off.
